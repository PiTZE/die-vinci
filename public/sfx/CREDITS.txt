Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio
Taken from dice-throw-1/2/3.ogg and dice-shake-1.ogg, downmixed to mono and
re-encoded to 32kHz 80kbps MP3, which every browser can decode. The originals
are Ogg Vorbis, which Safari cannot be relied on to play.

The throws play one per roll while rolls are slow enough to hear apart. The
shake is looped and pitched up underneath them once they are not.
